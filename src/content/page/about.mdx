      ## My approach

      My therapeutic approach draws on Bowen Family Systems Theory, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), and Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT). I also integrate practical skills from Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT) to support emotional regulation, self-understanding, and meaningful change. I believe that exploring our stories and patterns can help us understand ourselves more deeply, while developing new skills gives us tangible ways to move forward.
